## Executive Summary

Prior to remediation, all 26 published articles contained outdated "2024" references in titles, meta descriptions, and body content, despite being published/maintained in late 2026. Furthermore, multiple articles relied on unsubstantiated revenue forecasts (e.g., "$10k/Month Blueprint", "Up to $50/Click"), unsupported claims ("100% Proven Checklist", "Tested & Ranked" without testing logs), and thin content (< 800 words across 5 articles).

Every article and page has now undergone comprehensive remediation:
1. **Title & Temporal Modernization:** All titles and headings have been updated to remove artificial year-stamping while preserving original URL slugs to protect incoming links and indexing.
2. **Unsupported Claims Elimination:** "100% Proven" and "Guaranteed" claims have been removed and replaced with evidence-based policy compliance frameworks.
3. **Thin Content Expansion:** Articles 14, 16, 19, 20, and 27 were expanded from under 800 words to 1,350–1,500+ words each, adding comprehensive architectural breakdowns, retention curves, and procedural instructions.
4. **YMYL & Risk Disclosures:** Post 48 (Automated Crypto Trading Bots) was rewritten into an objective technical architecture review with prominent risk disclaimers and non-advisory language.
5. **E-E-A-T Architecture:** Author boxes linking to the editorial team profile and transparent methodology boxes have been appended across all articles.
